How the score is built

Five components, each scored out of 100 and then weighted. Nothing is hidden: every analysis shows you the same breakdown.

Keyword matching 30%

Which skills you evidence, and how many of the target role's requirements they cover.

Content quality 20%

Whether your bullets state outcomes with real numbers rather than listing duties.

Structure & format 20%

Whether the standard sections exist and are headed clearly enough to be parsed.

ATS compliance 20%

Contact details, section headings, action verbs, and a length a screener will read.

Experience relevance 10%

Career length inferred from stated tenure and the date ranges on your roles.

Your files stay on your machine

The analyzer runs entirely on the local API. Uploads are written to a temporary file, read once, and deleted immediately — nothing is stored and no account is needed. Optional AI enrichment is off unless you turn it on yourself, and it can point at a model running on your own computer.
