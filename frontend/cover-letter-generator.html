Writing Tips

Be Specific

Reference specific details from the job posting to show you've done your research.

Tell Your Story

Connect your experience to the company's needs. Explain how you can add value.

Show Enthusiasm

Let your genuine interest in the role and company shine through your words.

Proofread

Always check for grammar and spelling errors. A single mistake can hurt your chances.

Keep It Concise

Aim for 3-4 paragraphs total. Recruiters skim, so make every sentence count.

Call to Action

End with a clear next step, like "I look forward to discussing how I can contribute."
